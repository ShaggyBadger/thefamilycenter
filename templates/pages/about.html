About Us

Showing up today. Building toward tomorrow.

The Family Center brings the community together through events and practical support for local young people in Winston-Salem and Forsyth County, North Carolina.

Where the work is headed

The Family Center wants students to have support and opportunities to thrive in high school and pursue a future that fits them. More ongoing opportunities are a goal the organization is exploring — not a promise of programs already in place.

Our Vision

Every young person in Winston-Salem has the resources, relationships, and confidence to grow into a thriving adult. We imagine a community where opportunity is not determined by zip code, and every family has a seat at the table.

We connect families, neighbors, volunteers, and businesses through community events, practical support for students, and long-term programs that create lasting change. Every gathering, every supply drive, and every partnership moves us closer to a stronger community.

Our core values

Community first

We build bridges between families, volunteers, and local businesses. Every event and program is designed to strengthen the bonds that hold Winston-Salem together.

Integrity in every detail

We operate with transparency and accountability. Our review process ensures every program and partnership aligns with the best interests of local young people.

Growing together

We believe in progress, not perfection. As an organization, we are constantly learning, adapting, and expanding to meet the evolving needs of our community.

Our Story

Rooted in Winston-Salem, growing with the community.

The Family Center started with a simple belief: that a community is strongest when its young people are seen, supported, and celebrated. From the first neighborhood block party to today's expanding programs, every step has been guided by that belief and by the people who show up to make it real.

What began as volunteer-led gatherings has grown into a network of events, partnerships, and support systems that reach across Forsyth County. We are proof that small acts of community can add up to something lasting.
