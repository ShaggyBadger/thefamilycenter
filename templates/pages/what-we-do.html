What We Do

Bringing Winston-Salem families together, today and tomorrow.

The Family Center connects young people with the community events, practical support, and long-term opportunities they need to thrive right here in North Carolina.

Bring people together

Community events

From neighborhood block parties to seasonal fundraisers, our events give Winston-Salem families, neighbors, volunteers, and local businesses a chance to connect. Every gathering is designed to strengthen the bonds that make our community resilient.

See upcoming events →

Meet practical needs

Support for students

Backpack drives, school-supply kits, and holiday assistance programs help local students start the school year with confidence. The Family Center works directly with families across Forsyth County to make sure no child goes without the basics.

Get involved →

Build for the future

More consistent opportunities

Beyond events and supplies, The Family Center is growing toward ongoing programs that give young people year-round support — mentorship, tutoring, and pathways to higher education and careers in the Piedmont Triad region.
