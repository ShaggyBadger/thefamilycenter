# Project Commander's Intent

**Status:** Approved by the owner on 2026-09-25

Build and continually improve a trusted, accessible, mobile-first, locally discoverable website that helps The Family Center advance its purpose of preparing elementary- and middle-school students to enter high school ready to thrive. Make CEO-approved information about the nonprofit's verified mission, current work, events, and ways to connect easy for families and supporters to find and use, while keeping future aspirations distinct from current services. Give the organization secure, maintainable tools to grow the site over time without exposing draft content or collecting more personal information than necessary.
